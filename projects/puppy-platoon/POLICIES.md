**How to start a return**
Email us at numanshopify2006@gmail.com with your order number and the reason for the return. We will reply with the return address and instructions. Items sent back without contacting us first may not be accepted.

**Exchanges**
Need a different size? Tell us in your email and we will help you swap sizes.

**Damaged, defective or wrong items**
Email us within 14 days of delivery with a photo of the item and the packaging. We will send a replacement or a full refund at no cost to you.

**Refunds**
Once we receive and check your return, we will email you. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 5–10 business days. Your bank may need extra time to post it.

## Shipping policy

We ship to the United States and worldwide.

**Shipping options**
- **Free Standard Shipping** on every order. Estimated delivery: 5–7 business days.
- **Express Shipping**: $3. Priority handling and tracking.

Delivery times are estimates, start when your order ships and exclude weekends and public holidays. Remote areas and customs checks can add time.

**Tracking**
When your order ships, we email you a tracking link. You can also see your order status from the link in your order confirmation email.

**Customs, duties and taxes**
Orders shipped outside the country of dispatch may be subject to import duties, taxes or customs fees charged by the destination country. These charges are not included in the price or shipping cost and are paid by the recipient on delivery where applicable.

**Package Protection (optional, $3 per order)**
You can add Package Protection in your cart. If your protected package is lost, stolen or arrives damaged, email us within 14 days of the delivery date (or within 30 days of the latest estimated delivery date if it never arrived) with your order number, and a photo if it is damaged. We will reship your order or refund it. Without Package Protection, orders that are lost in transit are still handled according to this policy and applicable law.

**Wrong address**
Please check your shipping address at checkout. We cannot be responsible for orders sent to an incorrect address entered at checkout. Email us right away if you spot a mistake.
