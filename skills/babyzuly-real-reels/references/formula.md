# The cloudfel formula (from 20 agents analysing all 14 @cloudfel reels frame by frame)

@cloudfel sells "Reinfel", a plush baby-deer hooded onesie. They posted 14 reels in 16 days, all AI image-to-video clips that a human stitched in CapCut. The top reel got 19.2M views and 1.58M likes; the worst got 14K views. The per-reel teardowns are in `<your-project>/cloudfel/work/NN_<id>/ANALYSIS.md`.

## The one-line formula
**Hide the face behind a "toy" read → prove it's alive → slow turn → eye contact → a parent's hand.**

## Winning rules (ranked, with evidence)

1. **Hide the face for 4.5–6.5 s, then reveal it once.**
   - Top reel: eyes at 6.25 s → 19.2M. Reel 02: 4.75 s → 4.3M. Reel 03: 4.75 s → 1.1M.
   - No reel that showed the face at 0 s got more than 454K. Early leaks (a face at 3 s) hurt.
2. **End on a centred close-up of the baby's face looking into the lens**, with a micro-action: a blink, a smile, or a parent's hand on the cheek.
   - The last frame rhymes with the "toy face" at 0 s, which makes people rewatch.
   - Ending on a wide shot lost the payoff (140K).
3. **A real sun stripe across a white duvet, but with NEUTRAL white balance** (see realism.md). Every flat-grey-light reel stayed at 126K or below.
4. **The same set every time:** a white bed with a rumpled muslin duvet, an upholstered headboard, a small wall sconce and one velvet pillow. Moving to a crib, outdoors or a styled sofa cut reach 3–30×.
5. **High contrast:** a dark, saturated costume on white bedding. The light-fawn variant underperformed. For us: **Santa red or Gingerbread brown on white**. Ivory Snowman on white needs a cream or grey duvet.
6. **An infant of about 6–10 months, not a toddler.** The older standing toddler drew "AI" comments.
7. **3 shots, 12–14 s, cuts at about 2–3.5 s and 6–7 s:** hook → reveal → close-up payoff. Every single-shot reel under 10 s flopped.
8. **No on-screen text** (both reels with text were losers), or at most one misdirect line in the first 2–3 s that never spoils the twist.
9. **Music:** a soft lullaby / music-box bed at about 80–110 BPM, −14 LUFS, no voice. A coo or giggle lands 0.3–1 s after the reveal. Put the music swell on the eye contact, not before it.
10. **Post at 01:00–02:00 UTC, daily, and iterate on the winning template.** The account wandered (outdoor, crib, selfie, stroller) and every variant got ≤ 640K. Going back to the bed formula got 4.3M.

## Anti-patterns
- Showing the face at 0 s.
- Showing the adult hand before the reveal (it kills the toy illusion).
- Crib + flat grey light.
- A single shot under 10 s.
- Text questions that spoil the twist.
- Reusing old footage.
- A sleeping baby.
- Surreal props (a real fawn).
- An older toddler.
- Ending wide.
- Posting the same colorway or twist back-to-back.
- Pose teleports and the costume changing between clips (AI giveaways that rewatchers notice).

## BANNED for BabyZuly even though cloudfel did it
- A baby face-down or prone with its face in the bedding.
- The hood over the face.
- Eyes closed (reads as asleep).
- A baby standing or crawling (a 0–6-month garment, and the star legs can't crawl).
- A live animal touching the baby.
- No adult anywhere.

## Safe ways to hide the face (they replace cloudfel's face-down "plush")

| ID | Method | How |
|---|---|---|
| M1 | **Empty-garment match-cut** (safest, most honest) | Clip A: the empty finished star lies flat on the duvet like a toy. Hard cut to the same framing with the baby inside, on its back and awake. |
| M2 | **Back-view supported sit** (cloudfel's most-used beat) | The baby sits with mom's hand at its lower back, facing away. The hood and star arms read as a plush. The baby turns its head over its shoulder: cheek → eye → face. |
| M3 | **Held upright over the shoulder** | The parent holds the baby against their chest. The camera sees the back of the hood over the shoulder, then the parent turns. |
| M4 | **Crop then tilt** | Open tight on the body (buttons, belt, star points) with the face out of frame, then tilt up. This is the weakest version. |

## Reference structure: the BabyZuly 3-clip reel (≈ 12 s)

| Clip | Length | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **A, HOOK** | 0–3 s | Empty star on the duvet (M1), or the back-view sit (M2). Face hidden. A tiny "is it alive?" twitch (pom-pom wobble, one point lifts). |
| **B, REVEAL** | 3–7 s | The baby is inside / turns its head. The face appears at about 4.5–6 s. |
| **C, PAYOFF** | 7–12 s | The baby on its back or supported, face centred in the hood. Mom leans in. Eye contact, then one blink and a smile. Mom's hand enters **only now** and taps a garment detail (pom-pom, bell, peppermint, carrot nose). End on the smile so it loops. |
