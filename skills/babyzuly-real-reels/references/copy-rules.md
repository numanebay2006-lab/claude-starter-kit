# Honesty & safety copy rules (from agent 15)

## (e) Honesty and safety copy rules for captions, hooks and on-screen text

**Product honesty**
1. **It is a DIY KIT.** Every caption must state it within the first 2 lines, e.g. "DIY crochet kit · you make this ✨". The page says "DIY kit, not a finished garment".
2. **"What you'll make", never "what you'll get".**
   - Allowed: "Crochet this for their first Christmas", "Kit: yarn in your colorway", "Made by you in ~8–10 hours".
   - Banned: "Shop the onesie", "Comes ready to wear", "Order this outfit", "Ships finished".
3. **Do not claim a pattern, hook or buttons are included.**
   - The live page says you need your own hook, scissors and yarn needle.
   - Pattern: "contact us before ordering". Never write "pattern included", "complete kit" or "everything you need".
4. **Skill and time honesty.**
   - Allowed: "Advanced beginner", "about 8–10 hours".
   - Banned: "Easy!", "Make it in one evening", "No experience needed".
5. **Size.** "Fits approx. 0–6 months". Do not imply toddler sizes.
6. **AI disclosure.** The babies are AI-generated (as are the store photos).
   - Never present them as a real customer's baby or as a review ("my daughter in hers!").
   - Turn on Meta's "AI info" label for generated video.
   - Recommended small CapCut text: "illustrative · made with AI".
7. **No fake social proof or urgency.**
   - No invented sell-out counts.
   - Delivery: "Order early for Christmas"; any date must say "estimated".
   - Verify MERRY10 is live before mentioning it; it is not on the live page.
8. **Trademark.** Call the variant **"Pink"** or "pink starfish". Never "Patrick", "Patrick Star", SpongeBob or #patrickstar in captions or hashtags, and avoid the name in generation prompts too, because it can summon the cartoon. Also no Santa-brand or Elf-on-the-Shelf references.

**Safety (overrides any cloudfel copy)**
9. **Supervised, awake wear and photos only. Never for sleep.** Add a caption line, e.g. "For supervised, awake photos only, never for sleep."
10. **Banned words and framing.** The star shape resembles a sleep sack, so avoid any sleep association:
    - sleep, sleeping, nap, naptime, bedtime, sleepy, "sleep sack", "swaddle", "bunting", "sleeping bag", "cozy all night", "dreamy", "tuck them in", "lullaby"
    - a cloudfel-style "she fell asleep in it" punchline
11. **Visual rules restated for hooks.**
    - No sleeping or closed-eyes baby.
    - No face-down or prone baby.
    - The hood never over the face.
    - No loose pillows or blankets near the face.
    - The baby is on its back, in a supported sit, or held upright; a parent's hand is in frame or nearby.
    - The bed is a *photo set*, so stage it in daylight with the adult present.
12. **Fake-out hooks must not imply danger or neglect.**
    - Allowed: "Wait… that's not a toy 👀", "POV: the plush blinked", "Is it a cookie? Look closer", "I crocheted a starfish. Then this happened."
    - Banned: "Left my baby on the bed", "Found him like this", anything suggesting the baby was alone or hidden.
13. **Care line (optional, from the page).** "Check for loose yarn, buttons or trims before each use; never leave baby unattended."

For supervised, awake photos only, never for sleep.
Order early for Christmas (delivery dates are estimates) · link in bio
