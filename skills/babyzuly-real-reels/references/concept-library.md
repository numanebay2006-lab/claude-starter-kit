## Part 0: THE REALISM RULE (it overrides every concept below)

### 0.1 What every clip must look like
An unedited clip a mom shot on her phone and posted to her story. That means:

| Axis | REQUIRED | BANNED (never in a still or motion prompt) |
|---|---|---|
| Camera position | Adult eye or chest height: standing over the bed looking down, sitting on the bed edge, holding the phone at arm's length, or a POV from mom's own eyes or a selfie | Low "hero" angles, lens at duvet level, crane or gimbal moves, orbits, dolly |
| Framing | Imperfect and off-centre: the subject is 10–20 % off centre, the horizon tilted 2–5°, a bit too much headroom or a cropped star point, a thumb-edge shadow now and then | Perfect symmetry, a centred hero composition, rule-of-thirds polish |
| Movement | Hand shake, small re-frames, the phone moves closer when mom leans in (slightly uneven), a quick auto-exposure shift | "Smooth push-in", "slow dolly", "tracking shot" |
| Light | Plain daylight from a window, ordinary ceiling or table lamps, overcast outdoor light | Golden hour, "cozy glow", rim light, god rays, lens flare, "magical" |
| Color | **Neutral white balance**: whites look white or slightly cool. Phone HDR, normal saturation. Mixed WB in lamp-lit rooms (orange lamp + blue window or TV) | Warm grade, teal-orange, film look, "cinematic", "moody", "editorial" |
| Focus | Phone deep focus: the background is mostly sharp, with slight softness only very close up | Bokeh, creamy background blur, shallow depth of field, fairy-light bokeh balls |
| Image quality | Phone sharpening, mild noise (heavy noise at night), slight motion blur on fast hands, highlights slightly blown on a window or lamp | Clean "4K", "8K", "ultra-detailed", "masterpiece" |
| Set | A real lived-in home: a phone charger on the nightstand, a water glass, a burp cloth, a laundry basket, a baby monitor, a toy on the floor, a slightly messy duvet. **Clutter stays ≥50 cm from the baby's face.** | Styled set, a matching décor palette, a prop-perfect Christmas card, "luxury" |

### 0.2 Night tree scenes, specifically
- A phone in a dim living room.
- Visible grain or noise, especially in the shadows.
- The table lamp is slightly blown to white.
- Mixed white balance: orange lamp, cool blue from the TV or window, multicolour tree bulbs that show as small blown dots.
- The tree is partly cut off by the frame.
- Ordinary clutter: a couch throw, the TV remote, a kids' toy, a cardboard box.
- Skin tones look a little yellow/orange from the lamp. Slight motion blur when the baby moves.
- It must look like a real Tuesday-night clip, **not a Christmas card**.

### 0.3 Words for Higgsfield prompts

**Use these phrases (they push toward realism):**
- "casual iPhone video a mom took", "handheld at adult height"
- "slightly off-centre and tilted", "unedited", "phone auto-exposure"
- "neutral white balance", "plain daylight from the window", "ordinary ceiling light"
- "normal lived-in bedroom with a little clutter"
- "background in focus like a phone", "mild phone noise"

**Never write:**
- cinematic, film, filmic, moody, editorial
- golden hour, glow, cozy, warm tones, magical
- bokeh, shallow depth of field, lens flare, rim light
- 4K/8K, masterpiece, professional, studio, luxury, perfect

Do not even use these as negatives: naming them primes them. Use **only** the positive realism phrases above, plus one plain line: "no color grading, no beauty filter".

### 0.4 What we keep from cloudfel, and what we drop
- **Keep the structure:** a still object → evidence of life → a slow turn → eye contact → a parent's hand. It works independently of the polish.
- **Drop the polish:** cloudfel's low bed-level camera, luxury bedroom and hard raking sun read as staged. Our version is shot by mom standing or sitting at the bed, in a normal room, in plain window daylight.

## Part B: Safety and honesty translation

### B1. Unsafe cloudfel beats and their safe BabyZuly equivalents

| Cloudfel beat | Problem | BabyZuly safe equivalent |
|---|---|---|
| Face-down "plush" on the duvet | Prone | **Empty-garment illusion → match-cut.** The empty star suit lies flat on the bed; cut to the same framing with the baby on its back, eyes open. Honest, because the empty suit is what you make. |
| Crawling out of a lump | Prone | **Supported back-view sit.** The baby sits with mom's hand at its back, or sits on her lap. |
| Eyes shut / sleepy | Sleep framing | Eyes open throughout, at most one blink |
| Standing on a soft bed | Fall risk | Held upright by an adult |
| Hood slipping during the turn | Hood over the face | "the hood stays back, the whole face visible" |
| Pillows near the head | Loose items | Pillows and clutter stay ≥50 cm from the face |

### B2. Honesty rules
1. Each reel carries one kit signal: "you crochet it", "kit", "what you'll make" or "yarn set".
2. No claims that a pattern, hook or buttons are included, or that it ships finished.
3. Maker stories are written as **POV** or "what you'll make". No fake first-person "I made this in 3 weekends" over AI footage.
4. Turn on the platform's AI-generated label.
5. Never use "sleep sack", "sleeping bag" or "bunting". Never use the trademarked character name; customer-facing it is "Pink Star".

### C5. Phone-language camera vocabulary (the only moves allowed)
- **"standing over"**: mom stands at the bed and points the phone down at a 60–80° angle (not perfectly top-down), with small shake.
- **"sitting at the edge"**: mom sits on the bed with the phone at her chest height, angled slightly down at the baby.
- **"lean in"**: mom leans closer; the frame grows ~15 % unevenly, with a small auto-focus hunt.
- **"POV"**: the view from mom's eyes, her own hands in frame.
- **"selfie"**: front camera at arm's length; slight wide-angle face distortion.
- **"walking behind"**: mom follows 1 m behind and the image bobs with her steps.

### C6. CapCut
- Lowercase text, small, white with a light shadow, at 18–22 % from the top.
- Cut on the beat.
- 8–13 s for multi-clip reels, a 4–5 s loop for a single clip.
- No filters, LUTs or "warm" presets.

### Strength rubric

There are 5 factors. A concept scores 1 for each factor it meets fully and 0.5 for partial.
1. **Star silhouette in frame 1.**
2. **Delayed face reveal.**
3. **Eye-contact payoff plus a hand or laugh.**
4. **Comment trigger.**
5. **AI feasibility**: low motion, one baby, the garment facing the camera.

The total is rounded to give the 1–5 strength.

**Bedroom baseline for every "white bed" concept:**
- A normal bedroom in plain morning daylight from one window (neutral, slightly cool whites).
- A white or light-grey duvet, rumpled.
- A plain headboard or the wall.
- A nightstand with a phone charger, a water glass and a baby monitor.
- A laundry basket or a cardigan at the frame edge.
- The pillows pushed up at the headboard, far from the baby.

## Part E: The "simple idea → full concept" expansion method (skill spec)

**Input:** a one-line idea. **Output:** a concept block in the Part D format.

### Step 1: Parse into 5 slots
| Slot | Default |
|---|---|
| COLORWAY | Infer: snow → Snowman, tree → Tree, cookie/kitchen → Gingerbread, gifts/Santa → Santa, elf → Elf, funny/pink → Pink |
| SETTING | White bed, morning daylight |
| POSE | On the back, mom standing over |
| PARTNER | Mom's hand |
| PAYOFF | Eye contact → grin |

### Step 2: Pick one hook mechanism (the first match)
| ID | Mechanism | Trigger | Clip pattern |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Empty-garment illusion → match-cut | An object or lookalike | Empty garment → same framing worn |
| H2 | Back-view turn | A window, tree, view, "watching" | From behind → turn → face |
| H3 | Comparison meme | A real twin (snowman, cookie, tree) | The twin → tip down or cut → the baby |
| H4 | Recognition | The Pink colorway | One overhead loop plus a question |
| H5 | Maker reveal | Mom, gift, kit, grandma | Hands hold up or unfold → worn |
| H6 | Hand-boop loop | A detail (pom-pom, bell, nose, star tip) | Touch → laugh → reset |
| H7 | Carousel | Several colorways | 3 same-framing clips on the beat |
| H8 | Trend parody / POV | A December meme | Any of the above plus parody text |

**Rule:** if the mechanism isn't H1 or H2, add a 0–3 s face delay where possible (cloudfel's ~10× multiplier).

### Step 3: Realism pass (NEW, mandatory)
1. Camera: choose one of standing over, sitting at the edge, POV, selfie, walking behind, or a phone propped on furniture.
2. Framing: off-centre by 10–20 %, a 2–5° tilt, one imperfection (a cropped point, extra headroom, or a thumb-edge shadow).
3. Light: plain window daylight, ceiling or table lamps, or overcast outdoors. If night: dim room + noise + a blown lamp + mixed WB.
4. White balance: neutral. Lamp scenes are mixed, never "warm graded".
5. Clutter: add 2–4 everyday items (charger, water glass, monitor, laundry, remote, toy, slippers), all ≥50 cm from the face.
6. Strip every banned word (Part 0.3) and add the C3/C4 realism phrases.
7. People: real, unstyled (messy bun, pyjamas, hoodie, no makeup look).

### Step 4: Safety pass
1. Pose map:
   - lying → on the back, awake;
   - sitting → supported or on a lap;
   - standing or "by the window" → held upright;
   - sleeping → replace with "awake, blinking";
   - prone → replace with on the back.
2. The hood frames the whole face.
3. Nothing loose within ~50 cm of the head; flat bedding under the body only.
4. An adult hand, arm or lap appears in at least one clip.
5. Environment checks:
   - outdoors: dry, mild, short, held;
   - tree: no reachable ornaments or cords;
   - window: no leaning on the glass;
   - basket: low, with an adult hand on the rim.
6. If a check fails, change the POSE or PARTNER slot, never the rule.

### Step 5: Honesty pass
- One kit line in the text or the first caption line.
- Maker stories as POV or "what you'll make".
- No pattern, hook or buttons claims, no "finished", no sleep words.
- AI label on.

### Step 6: Beat map (1–3 clips, 4 s core each, one action + one phone move)
| Beat | Length | Job | Default phone position |
|---|---|---|---|
| HOOK | 2.5–4 s | The star silhouette or object; face hidden | Standing over, or sitting behind |
| REVEAL | 3–5 s | A kick, turn or match-cut | Same, with hand shake |
| PAYOFF | 3–4.5 s | Eye contact → grin + a hand on a garment detail | Sitting at the edge, lean in |

Use 8–13 s total, or a 4–5 s loop (start = end).

### Step 7: Setting presets (real-home versions)
| Setting | Light / WB | Clutter | Phone position | Safety |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| White bed, morning | Window daylight, neutral or cool | Charger, water glass, monitor, laundry | Standing over, sitting at the edge | Pillows at the headboard |
| Tree at night | Dim; blown lamp + TV blue + bulb dots; noise | Remote, toy, box, throw | Standing over, kneeling | No ornaments or cords in reach |
| Snowy window | Grey daylight, a blown window | Plant pot, sill dust, mug out of reach | Behind mom's shoulder | Held; no glass contact |
| Tree farm | Overcast midday | Mud, parked cars, netting machine, other shoppers | Walking behind | Dry, mild, short, held |
| Parent's arms | Matches the room | Hoodie or pyjamas, unmade bed | Standing, selfie | Upright hold; adult semi-reclined at most |
| Basket | Daylight or room lamp | Slippers, teether outside the basket | Standing over | Hand on the rim, no pillows |
| Made by mom | Ceiling + lamp, mixed | Laundry pile, yarn bag, TV remote | POV, propped phone | No hook or finger close-ups |

### Step 8: Write each shot
- **Still:** C3 base + preset + pose + partner + verbatim colorway description, with the C2 reference as a garment-only reference.
- **Motion (60–120 words):** "@image1 is the first frame, @image2 is the exact garment." + [one phone move in C5 vocabulary] + [one action as cause → effect] + the C4 lock.

### Step 9: Text, caption and audio
- **Hook text patterns:**
  - `wait… that's not a [x] 🫣`
  - `the cutest thing ever? 🫣`
  - `when the [twin] comes to life`
  - `who does she look like? 👀`
  - `POV: you [made] …`
  - `[character]'s newest [role]`
  - `forget [trend]`
  - `DON'T let a baby mom see this 😭`
- **Caption:** an emotional line + 🥹, then the colorway + kit honesty line, then one CTA, then 3–4 hashtags.
- **Audio:** a trending sound plus real room tone. A native giggle only as a low stem.

### Step 10: Score and upgrade
Score with the rubric. If the total is below 3.5, apply in this order:
1. Add a back-view or object opener.
2. Add a comment question.
3. Remove a prop or person.

**QA every frame for:**
- eyes open, the hood off the face, one baby, five fingers;
- garment details matching the reference;
- **no warm cast, no blur-bokeh, no centred hero framing.**

If a draft looks graded, **reject it**. Do not fix it in CapCut.
